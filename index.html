MOVE

Press Play to start.

Regular drops add 1 point; polluted drops subtract 1 point and may be ignored. Missed regular drops end the round. Drops fall at one speed. Normal drops spawn every 2 seconds; Thunderstorm spawns scoreable rainbow drops every 0.5 seconds for 10 seconds. The once-per-round storm protects you from missed drops and pauses polluted drops. The sky cycles day to night and back every 2 minutes. Dash recharges every 5 seconds. Music and sound effects can be muted.

Inspired by charity: water's mission to bring clean and safe water to people around the world.

Prototype game • No real donations or transactions
