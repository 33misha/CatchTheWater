Catch the drops

Catch regular drops and avoid polluted drops. Each missed regular drop uses one miss from your selected difficulty. Space dashes; Thunderstorm brings scoreable rainbow drops and protects you for 10 seconds once per round.

Use ← → or A / D keys. Press Space to dash.

Press Play to start.

Regular drops add 1 point; polluted drops subtract 1 point if caught and may be ignored. Miss limits are 3 for Easy, 2 for Normal, and 1 for Hard. Drop speed stays fixed and normal drops spawn every 2 seconds. Thunderstorm adds scoreable rainbow drops every 0.5 seconds for 10 seconds, protects you from missed drops, and pauses polluted drops. Milestone messages appear at 10, 25, and 50 points. The sky cycles day to night and back every 2 minutes. Dash recharges every 5 seconds. Music and sound effects can be muted.

Inspired by charity: water’s mission to bring clean and safe water to people around the world.
